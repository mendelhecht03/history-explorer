1 · Live sync on iPhone

  1. Install the free Userscripts app and open it once.
  2. Go to Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions › Userscripts, turn it on, and set All Websites to Allow. (Turn on Allow in Private Browsing too if you want those pages.)
  3. On this iPhone, in Safari, tap the button below. On the page that opens, tap the extensions button in the address bar, choose Userscripts, then Install.

2 · Upload a Safari export

Brings in everything Safari still remembers, and fills any gaps in live sync. Uploading again later is safe: duplicates are merged.
